Every finding from critiques, falsification audits, or external feedback is tracked here with a verifiable anchor back to the text. This is the "skip connection" — it prevents findings from being lost between discovery and implementation.

**Status lifecycle:** Pending (found, not yet addressed) → Landed (verified in text) / Rejected (deliberately, with rationale) / Diluted (addressed in form but not substance). A Landed finding can move to Diluted if subsequent iteration erodes the implementation.

**Severity:** Critical (factual error, credibility damage) > Substantive (content gap, dilution) > Editorial (craft, register, rhythm).
